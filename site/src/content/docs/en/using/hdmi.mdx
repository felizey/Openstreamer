---
title: HDMI output
description: Show the video on a TV, monitor or projector.
order: 40
---


Connect one or two screens before or after starting the Pi. Openstreamer shows the same
video and splash screen on both connected micro-HDMI ports by default. Under
**Display → HDMI → HDMI ports**, choose **Both HDMI ports**, **HDMI 0** or **HDMI 1**.
Video is decoded once and shared between the outputs. The current mode is listed
for each active screen. Connections are checked every second and display routes are rebuilt when screens change.

| Setting            | What it does                                                                                                            |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Show video on HDMI | Turn it off when nothing's plugged in: it frees the decoder and some CPU for streaming.                                 |
| Resolution         | **Automatic** picks a progressive screen mode up to 1080p, near 60 Hz. Or choose a standard manual preset or a mode reported by the screen. |
| Scaling            | **Fit** shows the whole picture, with bars if the shapes differ. **Fill** fills the screen and crops the edges.         |
| Rotate 180°        | For a screen mounted upside down.                                                                                       |
| Mirror             | Flips the picture left to right.                                                                                        |

The list always includes 31 standard presets, even without a detected screen.
It also includes additional modes reported by EDID. **p** means progressive frames;
**i** means interlaced fields, with the displayed rate in fields per second.
1080p60 and 1080i60 are separate timings. Standard interlaced presets cover
1080, 576 and 480 lines; 720p and 4K presets are progressive.
The aspect ratio note uses EDID physical dimensions, which a converter may report incorrectly.

The resolution setting applies to each screen. A manual preset requests its exact
standard timing, including 1920 × 1080p at 60.00 Hz. If the driver or screen rejects
it, select another mode or Automatic in the web interface. Scaling, rotation and
mirroring apply to both outputs. After HDMI cable changes, output is reconfigured
automatically. A supported stream without IDR receives a new decoder startup frame.
Reconfiguration can briefly interrupt both outputs.

While there's no video, HDMI shows the [splash screen](/docs/using/splash-and-logo).
