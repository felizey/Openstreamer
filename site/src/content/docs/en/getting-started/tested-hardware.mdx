The **Pi 4** column, the board revision, matters for DJI goggles: revision 1.1 boards have a USB-C
defect, see [early Raspberry Pi 4 boards](/docs/getting-started/requirements#cables).

Keep a record of your goggles and air unit, or USB camera, the Pi board revision and the result when testing this build.
